## Overview

**Noys** is a music video maker for creators and influencers: pick a template, add photos and a song, and the app turns them into a short music video or reel with transitions, effects and filters, ready to save in HD and share.

## What it does

- **Templates:** a large, regularly updated catalogue covering themes such as love, lyrics, birthday, festivals, beat, sad and happy, plus Bollywood-style AI clips.
- **Effects:** particle effects that react to the music's beats, and hundreds of filters and special effects.
- **Music:** a built-in free music library, or any song from the phone.
- **Slideshows:** merge any number of photos into a single video without losing quality.
- **Extras:** an MP3 cutter for custom ringtones and SMS tones, trending ringtones, and HD and quote wallpapers.
