## Overview

A holiday calendar with events, reminders and home-screen widgets.

## How it works

- Month view with national holidays for the selected country; add, edit and search events, synced with the device calendar.
- Interactive **App Widgets** (month grid with previous / next navigation, and an event list) that can be pinned from inside the app.
- Reminders use **exact alarms**, and the app listens for time-zone, date, locale and reboot changes to keep widgets and reminders correct.
