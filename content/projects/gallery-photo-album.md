## Overview

A photo & video gallery with albums, a zoomable viewer, a video player and crop / rotate tools.

## How it works

Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 on a data / domain / presentation architecture. It supports Android 14's **"selected photos only"** access, so it works even when the user grants partial media permission. Coil 3 and Media3 handle media; GDPR consent is managed with Google's UMP SDK.
