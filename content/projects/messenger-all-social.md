## Overview

One hub that opens your social, messaging and shopping sites inside a lightweight in-app browser, so you don't need a separate app for each.

## How it works

Jetpack Compose with Navigation for the UI, a WebView screen per service, Room and DataStore to remember your chosen apps, and a persistent quick-launch notification. It needs no SMS or telephony permissions.
