## Overview

An alarm clock that makes sure you actually wake up: alarms can require a task to dismiss them, such as a maths problem, a memory game or retyping a sentence.

## How it works

- **Exact alarm scheduling**, restored automatically after a reboot.
- Full-screen alarm over the lock screen, with snooze.
- Dedicated foreground services for alarms, the timer and the stopwatch.
- Reminders, bedtime and sleep music (Media3 ExoPlayer), world clock and themes. Room stores alarms.
